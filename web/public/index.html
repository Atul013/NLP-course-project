What’s behind the lens?+

Small model. Visible reasoning.

NLTK cleans the text. TF-IDF turns words and word pairs into features. Logistic regression compares those features with patterns learned during training. The highlighted terms show local weighted contributions, not causal explanations.

A course project, with clear limits.

Trained on 120 messages from a balanced, synthetic 200-message dataset. Validation used 40 messages and the held-out test used 40. The selected pipeline scored 1.00 test F1 on that small synthetic test; this does not establish real-world reliability. English only. Probabilities are uncalibrated.
